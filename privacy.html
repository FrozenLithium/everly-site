Privacy Policy

Everly, by WMD Corporations, Kolkata, India · Last updated 25 November 2026

Everly is a journal for two people. Each day it asks a question; your answer goes into your partner's diary. This page explains what Everly stores, who can see it, and how to remove it. Questions: contact@warismd.com.

What Everly stores

  • Account: your email address. If you sign in with Google, Google tells Everly your email address and name. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never stored in readable form.
  • Profile: the name you choose, an optional photo and your time zone (used so the daily question arrives at midnight where you live).
  • What you write and make: answers, notes, stories, Unsaid letters, shared plans, game rounds, shared canvas drawings and songs you choose to share.
  • Photos you add: location and camera details are removed before upload.
  • Notifications: a push token for each phone where you allow notifications, your notification settings, and a record of what was sent.
  • Safety: blocks and reports you make.

Who can see it

  • What you share is visible only to your connected partner. Everly has no public profiles, no ads and no social feed.
  • Care Notes and other private settings are visible only to you.
  • Notifications never contain the text of an answer or a note.
  • WMD Corporations does not sell your data and does not use it for advertising. Everly includes no analytics or advertising trackers.

What stays on your phone

  • Plan drafting: runs on your phone with a downloaded language model. The words you give it are not sent anywhere. The model file is downloaded from Hugging Face, which sees an ordinary download request.
  • Yearbook PDFs: made and kept on your phone. Everly never uploads or shares them for you.
  • Drafts of unsent writing are kept on your phone until you send them.
  • Dictation uses your phone's own speech recognition service (on most Android phones, Google's). Everly receives only the resulting text.

Services that process data for Everly

  • Supabase stores accounts, content and photos (servers in Sydney, Australia).
  • Google provides Google Sign-In and delivers notifications to Android phones (Firebase Cloud Messaging).
  • Expo relays notifications to Google's delivery service.

Songs you share open in your own music app; Everly does not stream audio.

How long it is kept

What you write stays until you delete it or delete your account. Notification delivery records are cleared after 90 days, and pairing attempts after 30 days.

Your choices

  • Delete individual entries at any time; for shared items, deleting removes them for both of you.
  • Export your data from Me → Export my data.
  • Disconnect from your partner, or block them, in Me.
  • Delete your account in Me → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, your answers and your diary.
  • Turn notifications off in Everly or in Android settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the date above changes.
